What this website collects

Nothing directly. This website has no accounts, no tracking or analytics, and sets no cookies of its own. Our fonts and photos are served from this website itself, not from third parties.

Hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes your IP address in its server logs to deliver the pages and keep the service secure (GitHub privacy statement).

Maps and directions

The map on our contact section only loads if you choose Show map. When you do, the map is provided by Google, which receives your IP address and may set cookies (Google privacy policy). The Get directions link opens Google Maps in the same way.

Phoning us

Tapping a Call button opens your phone's dialler. We only see what any caller shares, and we use it only to deal with your enquiry or booking.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Contact us on the phone number on our home page. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
