Common Questions

Everything you might want to know before booking.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome, though booking ahead is recommended for weekends.

How far in advance should I book for a colour?

We recommend booking 3-5 days ahead for colour appointments as they take longer.

What happens before a colour appointment?

We start with a consultation about your hair, your previous colour and the result you want. A patch test comes first, so give us a call and we'll arrange it ahead of your appointment.

Do you do children's haircuts?

Yes, we welcome clients of all ages. Tell us your child's age when you call, and let us know if it's their first salon haircut.

What payment methods do you accept?

Card, contactless and cash are all accepted.

Ready to book?

Call or drop by — appointments fill up fast on weekends.
